About DeepDefendAI

Enhancing Road Sign Recognition with Defensive Distillation for Autonomous Vehicles is a research project aimed at improving the robustness of deep learning models against adversarial attacks. By applying **Defensive Distillation**, we enhance the reliability of neural networks in real-world scenarios, ensuring safer and more accurate road sign recognition.

🔹 Objectives

  • Improve model robustness against FGSM and PGD attacks.
  • Compare baseline vs. distilled models in adversarial settings.
  • Provide real-time attack visualization through web-based tools.

🔹 How It Works

Our research applies **Defensive Distillation**, a technique that trains a student model using softened probabilities from a teacher model. This process makes the model more resilient to adversarial perturbations. The platform allows users to:

  • Upload road sign images and apply adversarial attacks.
  • Compare performance between baseline and distilled models.
  • Analyze model accuracy, robustness, and attack impact using interactive graphs.
